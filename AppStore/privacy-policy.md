# Privacy Policy — AICore Guitar Tutor

_Last updated: October 17, 2026_

AICore Guitar Tutor ("the app") is published by AICore LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles information.

## The short version
We don't collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, analytics or tracking. Free users see labeled sponsor cards (see below).

## Microphone
The tuner uses your device's microphone to detect the pitch of your guitar. Audio is analyzed in real time in your device's memory and is immediately discarded. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. The microphone is only used while the tuner or "Practice with mic" is on, and you can turn off access at any time in your device's Settings.

## Apple Music (optional)
If you tap "Use my Apple Music listening" and allow access, the app reads the songs you recently played to suggest styles and artists. This happens on your device; the list is not saved beyond the styles and artist names shown in the app, and nothing is sent to us.

## Sponsor cards
Free users see clearly labeled sponsor and affiliate cards. The app downloads the list of cards from our website; this request contains no personal information, and we don't track whether you view or tap them. Affiliate links open in your browser, where the linked store's own privacy policy applies.

## Data stored on your device
Your lesson progress, XP, learned and favorite riffs, music preferences, practice history and app settings are saved only on your device. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes this data.

## Purchases
Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Apple shares only anonymous information needed to confirm that a subscription is active. Apple's handling of your data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

## Children
The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and post the new version at this address.
