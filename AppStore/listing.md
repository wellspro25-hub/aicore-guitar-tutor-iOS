## Description (4,000 max)
Learn to play fingerstyle guitar — the beautiful, flowing style where your fingers pick the strings instead of a pick.

AICore Guitar Tutor takes you from holding the guitar for the first time to playing your first complete piece, one short lesson at a time.

BUILT-IN TUNER
• Accurate microphone tuner for standard tuning
• Auto-detects the string, or lock onto the one you're tuning
• Clear needle, "tighten / loosen" hints and a tap of haptic feedback when you're in tune

FAMOUS RIFFS & MELODIES
• 18 riffs to learn, from movie, TV and classical themes (Ode to Joy, In the Hall of the Mountain King, the William Tell galop, Dies Irae, Für Elise) to original rock, blues, metal, country and Latin riffs
• Hear any riff first, slow it down, loop it
• Practice with the mic: the app listens and lights up every note you hit
• Picked for you from the music you love — choose your styles or import your Apple Music listening

STEP-BY-STEP LESSONS
• Posture, hand position and the p-i-m-a finger system
• Thumb independence, pinches and arpeggios
• Your first chords: Em, Am, C and G
• Travis picking, the classic folk pattern
• Song study: "Morning Light," an original piece written for beginners
• Every pattern shown as easy-to-read tab with a one-tap play-along metronome

PRACTICE TOOLS
• Sample-accurate metronome with tap tempo and accented downbeats
• Practice timer with daily totals and a streak counter
• Lesson progress tracking

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account and no tracking. Sponsor cards are clearly labeled and never track you. The microphone listens only while the tuner or mic practice is on, and audio never leaves your iPhone.

FREE TO START
The tuner, metronome, practice timer, the first five lessons, seven riffs and a free daily riff challenge are included. Premium unlocks every lesson and riff and removes sponsor cards.

SUBSCRIPTION DETAILS
Guitar Tutor Premium is available as a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription, with a free trial for new subscribers. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel anytime in your Apple ID account settings.

## App Review notes
AICore Guitar Tutor teaches beginner fingerstyle guitar.

• The Tuner tab requests microphone access to detect the pitch of the guitar string. Audio is analyzed in memory on device and is never recorded, stored or transmitted. To test without a guitar, hum or play a tone near the device.
• Lessons 1–5, the tuner, metronome and practice timer are free. Lessons 6–12 require the "Guitar Tutor Premium" auto-renewable subscription (monthly or yearly). Tap any lesson with a lock icon, or Settings → Upgrade to Premium, to see the paywall. Restore Purchases is available on the paywall and in Settings.
• No account or login is required.
